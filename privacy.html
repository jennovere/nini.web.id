Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy describes how the website nini.web.id (operated under the NINI Copytrade brand) handles information. If anything here is unclear, contact the admin via the channel on the contact page.

1. This website is static

nini.web.id is a static website. It has no registration system, no login, and no contact forms. It does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or any identity document.

2. Cookies, analytics and delivery

This website does not use advertising cookies, advertising pixels or behavioural profiling. The site is delivered through Cloudflare: Cloudflare processes standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser headers) in order to deliver and protect the site, as described in Cloudflare's own privacy policy. Cloudflare Web Analytics may also be active on this site — it is cookieless, does not use cross-site tracking and does not build personal profiles.

3. When you contact us

Contact happens through external messaging platforms (for example WhatsApp). In that case your message and the data you choose to share are processed by that platform under its own privacy policy. We only receive what you send to the admin.

4. The trading software

The Nini Bot / NINI Follower application is installed and runs on your own device. You create and enter your own Binance API credentials; we never ask for your Binance password, seed phrase or private key. Binance account data shown on the public pages of this website is aggregated performance data that is published for transparency.

5. External links

This website links to third-party services (such as Binance and WhatsApp). Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or the service changes. The current version is always published on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: reach the admin through the official WhatsApp channel listed on the contact section.
