How It Works

  1. Create or use your own Binance account

    You register and hold the account yourself. Nini Bot never holds or receives your funds.

  2. Connect the account through API

    You create an API key in your Binance account and enter it into the application on your own device.

  3. Configure the copy-trading settings

    You choose the strategy, position sizing and risk limits. You stay in control of the configuration.

  4. The system executes trades according to the configured strategy

    Orders are placed through the API connection on your account, according to the settings you selected.

Your funds remain in your own exchange account at all times.

The software only places trades through API access. It never takes custody of deposits or withdrawals.

Non-Custodial & Security

Your funds, your account

  • Nini Bot is not a custodian of your funds.
  • Your funds stay in your own Binance account.
  • The system uses API access only to run the trading functions you configure.

What we never ask for

  • We never ask for your Binance password.
  • We never ask for your seed phrase or private key.
  • API keys are created and stored by you, on your own device.

Configure API permissions carefully

API permissions should be configured carefully by you. If your API configuration allows it, disable the Withdrawal permission and enable only the trading functions you need. Restricting the key to trusted devices or IP addresses, where Binance allows it, adds a further layer of protection.

Copy Trading

Copy trading helps you follow and replicate trading activity based on an available strategy. When the strategy opens or closes a position, the same type of trade can be executed on your connected account according to your settings.

Copy trading involves financial risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

You decide which strategy to follow, how much to allocate and when to stop. Historical results are published for transparency — you can review them on the public portfolio audit page, including losing periods.

About Nini Bot

What it is

Nini Bot (NINI Follower) is a desktop application that connects to your own Binance account through an API key you create, and executes futures trades according to the strategy and limits you configure. It runs on your own computer or VPS.

How it is paid

The service is provided for a flat monthly subscription. There is no profit sharing — your trading results belong to you, and the subscription price does not change with your profits or losses.
